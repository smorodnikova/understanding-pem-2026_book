# Understanding PEM
### What the Research Says in 2026

**The Manual Your Body Didn't Come With** — a synthesis of current research on PEM,
Long COVID, ME/CFS and POTS, by Jane Smorodnikova.

It reports what the published literature does and does not establish. It is a review
of published science, not a report of original findings, and it is not medical advice.
