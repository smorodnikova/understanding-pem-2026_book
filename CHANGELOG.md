# Changelog

The book reports a field that is moving quickly, and some figures in it will
date. Corrections and updates are listed here, newest first.

## v1.1 — 2026

Bibliography overhaul. Every citation in the book now carries a resolvable
identifier where one exists.

- **Removed a reference to a work that does not exist.** The book cited
  "Raj SR et al. (2018), Current concepts in the diagnosis and treatment of
  POTS, *Heart* 104(16)". No such paper exists; Raj SR has never published in
  *Heart*. Replaced with the 2015 Heart Rhythm Society expert consensus
  statement, which appears to be the source of the distortion.
- **Corrected an attribution.** A 2024 commentary on peripheral serotonin was
  credited to Afrin LB and Molderings GJ. Its authors are Anderson GM, Cook EH,
  Blakely RD, Sutcliffe JS and Veenstra-VanderWeele J.
- **Corrected a fabricated title and study description.** The Peter RS 2025
  *PLoS Medicine* paper was given a title that does not exist and described as a
  prospective cohort of 1,154. It is a nested case-control study of 982 cases
  against 576 matched controls.
- **Sourced a figure that had no citation.** The claim of 49 per cent higher
  healthcare utilisation and roughly thirty clinical contacts a year is from
  Lin L-Y et al. 2024, *BMC Medicine* 22(1):255 (OpenSAFELY, n=52,988).
- Corrected the locator on El-Sayed & Hainsworth 1996, the title on Bourne et
  al. 2021, and a van Campen entry that had been spliced from two papers.
- Replaced a medRxiv preprint citation with the published *Sports Medicine*
  version (Ruijgt et al. 2026).
- Standardised the Hitch & Wrench handbook, which had appeared under five
  different spellings.
- Added 216 DOI and PubMed links and 31 stable URLs. Replaced two dead links.
- Updated the RECOVER-AUTONOMIC ivabradine entry: the trial completed in
  December 2025.
