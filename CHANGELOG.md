# Changelog

The book reports a field that is moving quickly, and some figures in it will date.
Corrections and updates are listed here, newest first.

## v1.1 — 2026

Bibliography overhaul. Every citation that rests on a published study now carries a
resolvable identifier.

- **Removed a reference to a work that does not exist.** The book cited "Raj SR et al.
  (2018), Current concepts in the diagnosis and treatment of POTS, *Heart* 104(16)".
  No such paper exists, and Raj SR has never published in *Heart*. Replaced with the
  2015 Heart Rhythm Society expert consensus statement, which appears to be the source
  of the distortion.
- **Corrected an attribution.** A 2024 commentary on peripheral serotonin was credited
  to Afrin LB and Molderings GJ. Its authors are Anderson GM, Cook EH, Blakely RD,
  Sutcliffe JS and Veenstra-VanderWeele J.
- **Corrected a fabricated title and study description.** The Peter RS 2025 *PLoS
  Medicine* paper was given a title that does not exist and described as a prospective
  cohort of 1,154. It is a nested case-control study of 982 cases against 576 matched
  controls.
- **Sourced a figure that had no citation.** The claim of 49 per cent higher healthcare
  utilisation and roughly thirty clinical contacts a year comes from Lin L-Y et al.
  2024, *BMC Medicine* 22(1):255 (OpenSAFELY, n=52,988).
- Corrected the locator on El-Sayed & Hainsworth 1996, the title on Bourne et al. 2021,
  and a van Campen entry that had been spliced together from two different papers.
- Replaced a medRxiv preprint citation with the published *Sports Medicine* version
  (Ruijgt et al. 2026).
- Standardised the Hitch & Wrench handbook, which had appeared under five different
  spellings across the book.
- Added 216 DOI and PubMed links and 31 stable URLs. Two links that were about to be
  added turned out to be dead and were replaced before publication.
- Updated the RECOVER-AUTONOMIC ivabradine entry: that trial completed in December 2025.
